About Ashirwad

The Heart of Hazaribagh's Dining Tradition

Conveniently located at Bansilal Chowk / Kani Bazar Chowk in Hazaribagh, Ashirwad Restaurant has been a trusted dining destination for families, food lovers, and travelers. We take pride in serving wholesome North Indian tandoori delicacies, hearty curries, authentic Indo-Chinese starters, and freshly rolled breads.
